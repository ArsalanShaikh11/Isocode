1. System Architecture

CollectIQ replaces Company A's fragmented legacy workflow (daily overdue Excel exports, WhatsApp loan assignments, paper notebooks, and manual head-office compilation) with an integrated modular monolith architecture.

The application delivers two specialized experiences: a mobile-first Field Officer PWA with IndexedDB offline sync and 48px tap targets, and a desktop-first Branch Manager Terminal with live roll-rate analytics and CSV import.

2. Explainable AI Prioritisation Engine (0–100 Score)

The transparent scoring model evaluates urgency across four explicit components:

Scoring Component Weight Evaluation Criteria
Promise Integrity Max 30 pts Broken promise (+30 pts), Due today (+26 pts), Active pending (+12 pts)
DPD Roll-Rate Velocity Max 30 pts 15–30 DPD (+28 pts, nearing SMA-1 roll), 50–60 DPD (+30 pts, nearing SMA-2)
Outstanding Portfolio Exposure Max 25 pts ≥ ₹5.0L (+25 pts), ≥ ₹2.0L (+18 pts), ≥ ₹1.0L (+12 pts), < ₹1.0L (+6 pts)
Repayment Track Record Max 15 pts ≥ 3 missed EMIs (+15 pts), 2 missed EMIs (+10 pts), 1 missed EMI (+6 pts)

Benchmark Verification: Loan LN-11930 (Company D, Printing Press, DPD 19, ₹2.20,000, broken promise) scores exactly 86/100 with reasons: (1) Previously broken promise to pay; (2) Loan is 19 days past due, nearing roll into 31-60 DPD bucket; (3) Outstanding amount is ₹2.2 lakh.

3. Regulatory Guardrails & RBI Compliance

  • Contact Time Restrictions: Client and server validation enforce the 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST contact window under RBI Fair Practices Code. Blocked attempts create audit events.
  • Anti-Coercion Policy Guard: AI reminder templates in English and Hindi are filtered against prohibited terminology (police, jail, seizure, shame).
  • Advisory Model Governance: Algorithmic scores are advisory. Managers may override priority rankings with mandatory justification audit notes.

4. Offline Resilience (IndexedDB + Idempotency Keys)

Field officers can review cached assigned loans and record outcomes offline. Each outcome payload includes a cryptographic idempotency key (idemp-<timestamp>-<hash>) preventing duplicate submissions when background sync triggers.
